AstroDeck — Feature Guide

🚀 Getting Started

📍Click the location chip (top right) to set your city, lat/lon, and default viewing direction. The sky is rendered exactly as seen from that point on Earth.
🌍Three views: Solar System (3D orrery), Sky View (planetarium), Telescope Feeds (live imagery).
⏱The time bar at the bottom controls the simulation clock — pause, speed up, reverse, or jump to any date to see the sky in the past or future.

🪐 Solar System (3D Orrery)

🖱Drag to orbit the view · Scroll to zoom · Right-drag to pan.
🔭Click any body — planet, moon, or ISS — to focus the camera on it and see a live info card with distances, orbital data, and facts. The camera rides along as it moves.
🛸The ISS is a live 3D model: SGP4-propagated from today's TLE. Its solar arrays track the Sun in real time. Click it for ground track and pass info.
🌙12 major moons are shown when you focus their parent planet — Phobos, Io, Europa, Titan, Triton and more, all with real JPL Horizons orbital data.
🛰Satellites — the ~150 brightest known objects in orbit (Tiangong, rocket bodies…), SGP4-propagated from fresh Celestrak TLEs around Earth.
📡Starlink — the full SpaceX mega-constellation (thousands of satellites) as a live dense shell hugging Earth, propagated from current TLEs.
🔭Space telescopes — Hubble, Chandra, XMM-Newton and JWST as 3D models with their real orbits: Hubble's low circle, the wild multi-day ellipses of the X-ray observatories, and JWST riding Sun–Earth L2 with its halo ring. Optics correctly point away from the Sun.
✦Light ping — fire a pulse from the Sun travelling at real light-speed (in sim time); each planet lights up with its light-time as the wavefront passes. True scale — morph to honest sizes & distances and watch everything vanish into the void; a diameter banner appears and you can hover (or tap) any glowing marker — planets or the Sun — to magnify it against Earth. Halley's Comet rides its real retrograde orbit — time-machine to July 2061 for the tail.
🧲Mag fields — dipole field lines with each planet's real tilt (Earth 11°, Uranus a wild 59°). Venus and Mars correctly show none.
🌀Gravity grid — a spacetime sheet under the ecliptic; the Sun digs a deep funnel and every planet dents it, wells riding along as the planets orbit.
☀️The ☰ Controls panel (right side) has toggles for orbit paths, labels, the ISS, satellites, moons & rings (calms the blur at high time speeds), magnetic fields, the gravity grid, the Milky Way backdrop, and a sun brightness slider.
⏸Time bar: ⏮ slower/reverse · ⏸ pause · ⏭ faster · type a date and hit Go to jump there.

🌌 Sky View (Planetarium)

🖱Drag to look around the sky · Scroll to zoom in/out.
🎯Focus on… dropdown — pick any planet, Moon, or ISS and the view glides to track it. Live altitude ↑↓ markers show what's above/below the horizon.
🌒The Moon shows its real phase — crescent to full, lit from the Sun's true sky direction, with faint earthshine on the dark side.
🌌The Milky Way band arcs across the sky in its true position — brightest toward the Sagittarius core, with the Great Rift dust lane. Toggleable; fades out first in twilight.
✨Tonight panel — scans the coming dark window and lists every naked-eye planet + Moon: visible hours, peak altitude, compass direction. Tap any row to track it.
🛰️ISS pass alert — a banner appears only on nights with a genuinely visible pass (station >10°, sky dark, station sunlit). "Show me" jumps to the pass and tracks it.
🧭Live compass (bottom-left) shows your heading. Click it to snap back to North.
↺Reset View returns to your saved default direction and 28° altitude. Useful after manual panning.
🌅Atmosphere toggle adds day/twilight sky tinting and fades stars near the horizon. Off by default for pure star-gazing.

📱 Mobile

🔭Gyro / AR mode — tap the Gyro pill (iOS: grant motion permission when prompted), then hold your phone up and aim at the sky. The view follows where your phone points, compass-referenced.
🤏Pinch to zoom in Sky View and the Orrery.
☰☰ Controls pill collapses the right-side toggle stack — tap to show/hide.
⏱Tap the ⏱ chip at the bottom to show the time bar; tap again to fold it away for a full-screen view.

📍 Location & Settings

✏️Click the location chip (city name, top right) to open the location dialog. Enter coordinates manually or tap 📡 Use my location to pull GPS.
🧭Facing sets the default Sky View direction when you open the app or hit Reset View — N, NE, E … NW.
💾All settings are saved locally in your browser — no account needed.

🎯 Data Accuracy

🌍Planets: Schlyter Keplerian elements (arcminute-level). Moon: full perturbation series, ~0.02° vs JPL Horizons. 12 moons: JPL Horizons osculating elements. ISS: daily TLE + SGP4 propagation.
📡TLE fetched from wheretheiss.at on load with Celestrak as fallback. Refresh the page to pull the latest TLE for best ISS accuracy.
